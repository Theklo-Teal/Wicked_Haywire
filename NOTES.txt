Making technology visible, so it's understandable.

Device panel design:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neumorphism

Gizmo ideas:
	Info Panel/Note - point to a joint and show text
	Ammeter - Count Gizmos between two joints.
	Diode - Acts as HiZ in one direction, but allow signals in the other
	Hi-Z Switch - Disable pin sets connections to Hi-Z
	Monostable - A pulse flips output, which then returns after a set time.

Cabling/Bundle/Combiner/Splitter Is implemented as a Via connected to a bus and relays to other wires
certain bits specified in options. Effectively wire taps.
